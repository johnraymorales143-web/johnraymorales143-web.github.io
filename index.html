      05 · Questions

      Before you hire.

      I have a script. What happens next?

      Send it over with your product page and any references. I’ll storyboard it, lock the characters and product, then generate and edit the full ad.

      Are these real actors?

      No. Creators and characters are AI-generated, then locked with master images and a fixed voice so they stay consistent across every scene and hook.

      How many hooks do I get?

      Usually three to five openers on one body. You can see full hook sets in the reel and the French UGC work.

      Can you make ads in other languages?

      Yes. This portfolio includes ads in French, German and Dutch, with native scripts and captions, not just a translated voiceover.

      Why Claude MCP?

      It keeps the whole job in one thread. Claude remembers the brief, angles and locked sheets, and through MCP it sends generations and deliveries straight to the tools. Fewer mistakes, faster revisions, consistent variants.

      Can I download the videos here?

      No. Samples are view-only. Clients get full-resolution files in a private Drive folder.
